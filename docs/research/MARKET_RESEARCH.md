# Market research snapshot — 2026-08-30

## What changed in the category

Native spreadsheet products now include substantial AI functionality. Microsoft describes Copilot in Excel as able to build and edit workbooks, create formulas, charts, and PivotTables. Google continues adding AI-assisted spreadsheet creation, analysis, and complex-formula support. This makes formula generation and generic spreadsheet chat increasingly commoditized.

Dedicated vendors have moved up the value chain:

- Better Analyst, formerly Formula Bot, markets upload/connect, insights, charts, reports, and over one million users.
- Ajelix markets autonomous agents that complete whole spreadsheet tasks, create templates, visualizations, and financial models.
- Rows markets plain-language reports, connected data, and reusable/automated analysis.
- ExcelErrorFinder emphasizes free, no-signup, browser-only private audits.
- SaferSheets emphasizes pre-send audits, exact cell evidence, ranked findings, and annotated output.
- Tracelight emphasizes contextual model auditing, automatic fixes, zero-retention messaging, and enterprise security claims.
- PerfectXL and other professional audit tools demonstrate durable demand for spreadsheet risk management.

## Strategic interpretation

The opportunity is not to out-chat Copilot or Gemini. A focused entrant can win on a workflow that incumbents do not package clearly:

1. Independent pre-send diagnosis.
2. Evidence at rule/sheet/cell level.
3. Classification of what is safe to change.
4. Exact repair scope and price before payment.
5. Separate repaired copy and change log.
6. Privacy-first quick scan and short retention.
7. Human escalation for complex files.

## Success-case lesson

Formula Bot began as a narrow formula generator and later expanded. A 2025 founder interview reported more than $42,000 monthly revenue and approximately $500,000 ARR; the product now claims over one million users. The lesson is not to clone its current broad platform. It is to start with one urgent, legible job and use free utilities/organic acquisition to create a large top of funnel, then expand only after demand is visible.

## Design trend interpretation

The strongest current product pages show:

- Outcome-led headline.
- Immediate interaction or example.
- Low-friction free start with no card.
- Progressive disclosure rather than a dense feature menu.
- Trust and privacy near the primary action.
- Concrete outputs: reports, charts, fixes, annotated files.

For WorkbookCare, the product result itself should be the visual demonstration. Generic AI art would reduce credibility.

## Assumptions requiring validation

- Korean users will pay per file rather than subscribe.
- A free scan creates sufficient trust to upload a real workbook.
- Exact quote-before-payment is a differentiator rather than friction.
- Browser-only quick scan improves conversion materially.
- Basic deterministic repair is valuable enough before broad AI analysis exists.
- Existing Excel consulting demand can seed the first paid beta.
